The Course and the Competencies

The A Senior Course prepares coaches to lead player and team development and performance across a senior competitive season. Coaches develop and demonstrate the nine competencies together through their coaching practice with the players in the team they coach. The in-person meetings take that practice onto the field with other coaches and Coach Educators, and each coach’s own work is the starting point.

One Coaching Process Across the Course

From individual coaching interactions to the competitive season.

The three in-person meetings provide opportunities to explore and apply these interconnected capabilities in increasingly complex coaching situations, from individual coaching interactions to connected development cycles and the broader competitive season.

The Meeting Arc

At each meeting you coach a moment within a principle of your game model, with a player focus.

1

IPM 1: Teaching Plan and Interactions

Bring two players from the team: one whose qualities help the principles of your game model come to life, and one whose qualities called for an adaptation or a different expression of the game model. Design two activities within one principle that highlight the qualities of both players and show your player development plan and coaching approach.

2

IPM 2: Plan, Adjust, Reflect

Bring an Identified Game Pattern from the players in the team, with clips, measures and a planned session. At the IPM you coach players you do not know, so you profile them first, adjust the plan to fit them and reflect on how well the session met their needs.

3

IPM 3: Account and Transfer

Explain your season, coach a problem from it live and reason across seasons with peers. Consolidate your learning and carry it beyond the course. Cycle 3 provides a culminating opportunity to demonstrate integrated coaching capability and reflect on your development across the season.

Assessment Sits Outside the Meetings

The in-person meetings are not assessed. The Coach Educator assesses the coach’s work through the assignment submissions and the individual meeting after each one. Cycle 3 serves as the culminating assessment opportunity.
