IPM 1

Teaching Plan and Interactions  ·  College Jan 25–28  ·  Pro Jan 11–14, 2027

Focus

IPM 1 starts from two players in the team you coach and one principle of your game model. You show how each player’s qualities and strengths connect to the principle, then design and coach two activities that bring out the qualities of both players and show your player development plan and coaching approach.

Before IPM 1

  1. 01

    The first player

    Identify a player in the team and how the player’s qualities and strengths help the principles within your game model come to life.

  2. 02

    The second player

    Identify a second player whose qualities and strengths required an adaptation, or a different expression of the game model from the one you prefer, because of what the player can do.

  3. 03

    Two activities within one principle

    Design two activities within one principle of your game model that highlight the qualities of both players and show your player development plan and coaching approach.

1
Player one

The player’s qualities help the principle come to life the way the game model expects.

2
Player two

The player’s qualities called for an adaptation or a different expression of the same principle.

How IPM 1 Connects to the Assignments

A1

Both players come from the roster review and the player development process in A1.

A2

The A2 brief asks for the same work: “With two players from the A1 roster review, explain how each player’s qualities influence how a principle is expressed, including where the player’s strengths open a different solution within the same principle.”

A4

Each micro-cycle asks for a teaching plan and a practice approach for each activity, and for the work with the focus players. IPM 1 is the first time the coach plans both, across two activities.

In development

The IPM 1 week schedule, session planner and Coach Educator guidance are still being developed. The page fills in as each part is completed.

Assessment

IPM 1 is not assessed. The Coach Educator assesses the coach’s work through the assignment submissions and the individual meeting after each one.
