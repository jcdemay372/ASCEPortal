The Course and the Competencies

The A Senior Course prepares coaches to lead player and team development and performance across a senior competitive season. Coaches develop and demonstrate the nine competencies together through their coaching practice with the players in the team they coach. The in-person meetings take that practice onto the field with other coaches and Coach Educators, and each coach’s own work is the starting point.

One Discipline Across the Course

Identify, respond, measure.

Identify the behaviors your players need, respond through a teaching plan and coaching interactions, and measure your influence on their development and performance. The three in-person meetings apply it at increasing scale: a single coaching moment, then a cycle, then a whole season.

The Meeting Arc

At each meeting you coach a moment within a principle of your game model, with a player focus.

1

IPM 1: Teaching Plan and Interactions

Develop and implement a teaching plan from an activity you design before arriving. Build the foundation for each cycle by turning a plan into coaching that influences individual players within a connected group.

2

IPM 2: Assess and Adjust

Analyze your Cycle 1 evidence and adjust your coaching live in response to the profiles and qualities of the players on site. Strengthen your ability to read evidence, respond to the players in front of you and influence your next cycle.

3

IPM 3: Account and Transfer

Explain your season, coach a problem from it live and reason across seasons with peers. Consolidate your learning and carry it beyond the course.

Assessment Sits Outside the Meetings

The in-person meetings are not assessed. The Coach Educator assesses the coach’s work through the assignment submissions and the individual meeting after each one. Summative assessment comes through micro-cycle 3 feedback.
