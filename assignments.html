The Work

Four assignments

Your assignments are connected learning experiences, and each one builds on the one before it. They help you understand the players in the team, establish how you will support their development, put your game model and periodization into operation, and examine your influence across a competitive season. Select an assignment to open its full brief.

How the Assignments Connect

Integrated submissions across the four briefs

Each assignment connects to every other assignment. A1, A2 and A3 establish the foundations for applied coaching and connect with each other, and A4 brings the player development process, game model and periodization plan together in practice across three micro-cycles. Read across a row to see how that brief integrates with each of the others.

A1, A2 and A3 are formative and foundational: they establish the foundations for applied coaching. In A4, Cycles 1 and 2 are formative; Cycle 3 is summative and provides the final opportunity within the course to demonstrate your coaching practice and development. All four assignments are required for completion. They are developmental milestones, assessed together as one body of evidence.
